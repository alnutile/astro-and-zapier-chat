We covered a lot of ground, including:

→ Anthropic’s new Cowork desktop agent and why I think they really “get it” when it comes to practical AI tools

→ The rise of “micro apps” — how non-developers are now building their own tools instead of buying off-the-shelf software

→ Real-world AI automation strategies for businesses of all sizes

→ How to identify the right tasks for automation (spoiler: it’s not about automating everything)

One thing I emphasized: AI works best as a “side dish” that enhances existing workflows, not as the main course that replaces everything overnight. The key is finding those repetitive, time-consuming tasks that drain your energy and letting AI handle them so you can focus on what matters.

Big thanks to Jason for having me on — always a great conversation.
