Welcome back to the weekly roundup of no-code and automation news! This week, I’m diving into some game-changing updates that have been catching my attention across the no-code landscape. From Zapier’s enterprise-level AI features to Make.com finally adding code nodes, there’s a lot happening in the automation world.

I’ve been primarily working with n8n, but this week’s announcements have me seriously reconsidering my toolkit. Zapier is making some bold moves with their Copilot Assistant, Softr is bringing AI deeper into their database platform, and there’s even a new player called Kestra that’s taking a YAML-based approach to automation. Plus, we’ll explore how Claude Code is evolving into a full agentic framework—not just for coding, but for running long-term automated tasks.

Whether you’re a no-code builder, a developer looking to speed up your workflow, or just someone curious about where automation is headed, this episode has something for you. I’m also sharing why I’m planning a comparison between n8n, Zapier, and Make.com, because it’s time to make sure I’m choosing the right tools for my clients—not just the ones I’m comfortable with.

💡 Why This Matters:

The no-code and automation space is evolving rapidly. We’re moving from simple app-to-app connections to orchestrating intelligent AI agents that can work autonomously for hours or even days. The tools I covered this week—Zapier’s Copilot, Softr’s AI Agents, Claude Code’s agentic capabilities—all point to a future where building powerful automations doesn’t require deep technical knowledge.

But here’s the key: staying open to all these tools is essential. I’ve been comfortable with n8n, but Zapier and Make.com are making moves that I can’t ignore. That’s why I’m planning a comprehensive comparison of n8n, Zapier, and Make.com—including pricing, features, and real-world use cases. I want to make sure I’m choosing the right tools for my clients, not just the ones I know best.
