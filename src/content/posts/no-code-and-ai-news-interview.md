Had a great conversation with Darragh Mc Kay, founder of Noloco (YC S21), on The Daily AI Studio this week.

We got into some stuff I don’t hear discussed enough:

→ Why the no-code world of 2024 has completely collided with AI-first no-code in 2025

→ The Agency Operating System — a full CRM, PM, and client portal in one

→ Agentic workflows inside no-code tools — what’s actually coming vs. what’s hype

→ Vibe coding and why it’s raised the bar for every tool in the space

→ The honest case for no-code over code for small businesses

One line that stuck with me:

“If you don’t have the in-house skills to host and change the code yourself, don’t rush into it. Rely on off-the-shelf tools that can be made fit to your needs.”

Simple. But most small businesses still get this wrong.
